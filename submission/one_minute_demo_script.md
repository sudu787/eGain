# One-minute demo script

**0:00–0:06 — Set the problem**  
Narration: “During an outage, people need a safe next step and a way to report the problem. GridGuide handles both, with eGain as the customer-facing agent.”

**0:06–0:19 — Grounded answer**  
Ask: “How long will food stay cold if the power goes out?”  
Show the agent’s answer from the FDA-sourced knowledge article and source reference. Point out the estimate is qualified, not a guarantee.

**0:19–0:28 — Distinctive triage**  
Ask: “There’s a downed wire near my house.”  
Show the safety-first branch: do not touch or approach; contact local emergency services for immediate danger; routine report intake is bypassed.

**0:28–0:50 — Workflow and action**  
Start “Report an outage.” Choose “North Ridge” and “no power.” Confirm only after the agent states this writes to the fictional demo system. Show the successful API receipt and `GG-` reference.

**0:50–1:00 — Trust boundary and close**  
Show the demo-only notice and both fictional escalation options. Narration: “The report is persisted in the demo database, not sent to a utility. GridGuide knows what it can do—and when a person or emergency service is the right next step.”

Record only after the eGain connector and published experience are actually working. If the action is not available in the trial, do not stage a fake success; disclose that the action integration is pending and revise the video to show verified capabilities only.
