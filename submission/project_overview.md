# GridGuide — project overview

**Industry:** Electric utilities (fictional demonstration)  
**Audience:** Residents reporting a power interruption or seeking safety guidance  
**Primary channel:** eGain AI Agent customer self-service  
**Distinctive capability:** impact-aware triage that surfaces immediate safety needs before routine reporting, collects only a broad demo service area and issue category, and hands urgent or uncertain cases to a person.

## Customer problem

During a power interruption, people often need two things at once: clear, reliable next steps and a quick way to notify the service provider. A generic FAQ bot can answer questions but leave the user to determine the next action or repeat information during a handoff.

## GridGuide experience

GridGuide uses a small set of original, source-linked safety articles. It can guide a resident through a report workflow and, when connected, create a persistent fictional report in its supporting API. Every receipt clearly says the report was stored only in the demo system. The sample status feed is labeled synthetic and is never described as live.

## Value hypothesis

GridGuide aims to reduce effort for routine outage reporting while making safety escalation easy to reach. No measured service outcomes are claimed; any business impact must be validated with a real utility and operational data.

## Current delivery state

The supporting API, tests, knowledge content, intent/workflow specifications, and submission materials are prepared locally. eGain account configuration, API connectivity, publication, shareable demo URL, and eGain end-to-end test results are pending account access.
