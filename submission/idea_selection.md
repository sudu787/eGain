# Idea selection

Scores use a 1–5 estimate. Weighted score = impact × 25% + feasibility × 20% + eGain fit × 20% + differentiation × 15% + actions and judgment × 15% + demo potential × 5%.

| Education financial-aid guide | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3.45 |

GridGuide wins on urgent customer value, a clean fit for eGain knowledge and guided workflows, and a visible action that can be demonstrated without claiming access to a real utility. It also has a strong trust story: priority-based escalation and explicit separation of demonstration data from live service.

Rejected ideas remain plausible but were weaker for this constrained demo: cyber, healthcare, banking, insurance, and benefits raise higher legal or safety stakes; travel and telecom often need live provider integrations; retail is easier to implement but less distinctive; education aid has less immediate action value.
