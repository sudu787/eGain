# Submission description (134 words)

During an outage, residents need reliable safety guidance and a clear next step—not another maze of phone menus. GridGuide is a customer-service AI agent for a fictional electric utility, built around eGain knowledge, intent-connected workflows, and human escalation. It answers outage-preparedness questions from source-linked guidance, routes urgent hazards to safety instructions before routine intake, and guides residents through a short outage-report process. With explicit customer confirmation, its supporting API writes a synthetic report and returns a reference; the agent clearly explains that the record remains in the demo system and is not sent to a utility. GridGuide collects only a broad demonstration service area and issue category, never account or medical details. It does not claim live outage status or restoration estimates, and offers two clearly fictional contact options when people need human help.
