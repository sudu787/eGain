# Outage status and support options

GridGuide has no connection to a live utility system. Any outage list or report lookup shown in this demo uses synthetic records and is not a verified outage, crew assignment, restoration estimate, or real customer report. GridGuide must say it cannot confirm live status and must not estimate when power will return.

Fictional demo escalation choices:

- Email: **help@gridguide.example**
- Phone: **+1 (202) 555-0140**

These are reserved demonstration details and are not monitored support channels. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services instead.
